## The guided tour

The first time the main window opens, a four-step tour starts. You only
see it once.

1. **Cosa succede** (what happens): what the program will and will not do,
   before it does anything. It also checks whether your computer can run the
   models, and offers a trial on a fake library so you can try everything
   without risk.
2. **La cartella** (the folder): choose where your photos are. The program
   counts them and tells you what it found and how long it will take. The
   button that starts the import appears only **after** the count.
3. **L'elaborazione** (processing): the import runs while you watch, and the
   thumbnails appear as they are made. Here you can also download the AI
   models and compute the *fingerprints* the suggestions are based on. You
   can close the program halfway: next time it resumes from the next file.
4. **Si comincia** (let's start): it takes you to the first shot to label.

## Labelling

Every shot gets three possible answers: **yes**, **no** and **I don't know**.
"I don't know" is a real answer, and the program respects it instead of
insisting.

At the beginning the program suggests nothing, and that is correct: it has
not seen any of your decisions yet. After a handful of shots it starts
proposing labels, and every suggestion comes with its reason, made of real
shots you can open: "it looks like five shots you confirmed". The keys to
press are written inside the buttons, so there is nothing to memorise.

## The menu

| Menu | Meaning | What you do there |
|---|---|---|
| **Foto** | Photos | the gallery with filters, **Persone** (people) and **Luoghi** (places) |
| **Lavoro** | Work | **Etichetta** (label), **Importazione** (import), **Albero** (the category tree), **Da guardare** (to review), **Doppioni** (duplicates) |
| **Stato** | Status | **La pagella** (the report card), **La misura** (how accurate it is on your photos), **La salute** (models, backups, disk space) |
| **Telefono** | Phone | prepare the encrypted package for the phone app |
| **Due computer** | Two computers | move your work to another PC with a USB stick |
| **Impostazioni** | Settings | version, updates, AI engines, Hugging Face key, keyboard shortcuts |

## Good habits

- **Keep a backup of your photos.** The program never changes the originals,
  but no program replaces a backup.
- **Label a little, often.** The report card (*La pagella*) always tells you
  which category would improve the most if you labelled a few more shots.
- **You can reorganise your folders whenever you like.** The program
  recognises every file by its content, not by its path, so moving photos
  around does not lose their labels.
